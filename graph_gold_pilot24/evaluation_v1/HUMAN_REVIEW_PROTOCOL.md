# Content review before creating a real Gold Dataset

Schema approval on 2026-10-21 is not approval of any annotation. All 24 documents remain AI_DRAFT and NOT_REVIEWED; the gold directory stays empty. Structural tests and AI re-reading do not supply human truth or measured semantic recall.

1. Assign two technically qualified human annotators and an adjudicator. Record their actual identities/roles, dates and ontology hash only after they perform the work. Start with CALIBRATION to practice the fixed rubric and record interpretation disagreements. Keep the 23 relation types, domains/ranges and qualifier schema immutable.
2. Give each annotator the full frozen abstract chunks and annotation rubric. Prefer an initial independent pass without seeing AI suggestions to reduce anchoring. Each annotator then audits every sentence for missed entities/claims as well as false positives. Unannotated text and abstentions are not negative examples.
3. Review mention boundaries, material versus device identity, electronic/interface morphology, chemistry, composition, source-local aliases and cannot-link constraints. Review every assertion's endpoints/direction, exact supporting quote, author modality, negation scope, analyte, configuration, condition, comparator, numeric value/unit and qualifier attachment. A source-supported hypothesis remains PROPOSED rather than established physics.
4. Compare the two independent annotations. Report exact-span/entity-type agreement, identity agreement, canonical relation agreement, and separate agreement on modality, polarity and qualifiers. Count unique qualified claims separately from repeated evidence mentions; report raw disagreement counts alongside any aggregate score. Canonical triples alone do not make claims with different conditions or negation equivalent.
5. Adjudicate every disagreement with the source visible. Use ACCEPT, CORRECT, REJECT or ABSTAIN with human rationale and exact evidence. In particular, inspect every AI correction/new assertion and every documented abstention, including unsupported numerical qualifiers. A missing ontology representation stays abstained; log a future-version issue instead of adding a hidden qualifier or retyping an entity merely to fit a predicate.
6. After calibration review, review DEV and then HOLDOUT under the same locked scheme. Keep HOLDOUT tasks, labels, evidence, human disagreements and adjudication in closed storage accessible only to authorized reviewers; public summaries expose counts/status only. Do not tune systems or the ontology against HOLDOUT, and keep subsequent benchmark evaluation access separated from annotation staff where feasible.
7. Mark only the actually reviewed units/documents with real human review records. Promote labels into a separate human-gold release only after all relevant sentence coverage, evidence, disagreement decisions and structural checks pass. Track a complete lineage from AI draft to each human decision; schema approval must never automatically promote labels. Export public DEV/CALIBRATION gold separately from closed HOLDOUT gold, with immutable hashes and a release manifest.

No A/B/F/C/D experiments are part of this procedure or the current migration. A later experiment stage requires a separately accepted gold release and protocol.
